The Framework

Six Dimensions. One Complete Environment.

Sleep quality is not governed by a single variable. The SPA Standard measures performance across six scientifically defined dimensions — each with thresholds derived directly from peer-reviewed research.

Luminous Environment
Circadian-appropriate lighting design, blackout integrity, and elimination of intrusive light sources.
Acoustic Environment
Noise control informed by WHO Night Noise Guidelines, targeting levels below the biological stress response threshold.
Thermal Environment
Supporting the ~1°C core temperature drop required for sleep onset through precise climate control and airflow design.
Air Quality
Ventilation rates, CO₂ levels, filtration standards, and elimination of sleep-disruptive chemical or particulate irritants.
Postural Support
Medium-firm mattress performance, pillow configuration, pressure distribution, and maintenance protocols.
Electromagnetic Environment
Precautionary minimisation of unnecessary EMF exposure in the sleep zone as part of a comprehensive wellness approach.

Certification

Three Tiers. One Clear Path.

SPA certification is designed to meet properties where they are. From foundational sleep protection to optimised recovery environments, the framework defines three tiers — each with progressively more stringent, independently verified thresholds.

Sleep Friendly™
Eliminating Harm
A science-informed baseline that removes the most common environmental disruptors of sleep. The foundation for any property serious about guest rest.
Gold Recovery™
Supporting Recovery
Environmental parameters calibrated to more stringent thresholds — actively optimised to support the body's natural restorative processes.
Platinum Regen™
Peak Regeneration
Gold Recovery standards plus dedicated Sleep Suites equipped with evidence-based sleep enhancement technologies. The highest achievable standard.
